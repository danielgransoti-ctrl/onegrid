02 The experience

You feel it before you see it.

Motion

The platform reacts. So does your body.

The platform reacts in real time to what happens on track: the dive under braking, the curb kicking back, the rear sliding on corner exit. That is the difference between watching and driving.

  • 2–3 DOF electromechanical actuators
  • Traction loss on corner exit
  • Anchored base — no wobble, no flex

Controls

A wheel that hands the track back to you.

Direct drive with a quick-release hub: torque reaches your hands with no belt, no gears, no delay. The pedals are load-cell — you brake by pressure, not by travel — exactly like the real car.

  • Direct-drive wheel, race torque, quick release
  • Load-cell pedals, pressure-sensitive braking
  • On-wheel display and race controls

Immersion

The whole track in your field of view.

A 49" curved screen or a triple setup wrapping the cockpit, with positional audio. The hardware disappears and only the track is left — for you and for anyone watching.

  • 49" curved or triple configuration

04 Engineering

Under the bodywork.

Structure, body, motion and electronics are designed together to take heavy use and stay precise. Tap the points to look inside.

  1. 01

    Motion actuators

    Fast-stroke electromechanical actuators drive the platform through 2–3 degrees of freedom, synchronized with the simulator's physics.

  2. 02

    Structural chassis

    A rigid dedicated frame. It is what turns wheel force into sensation instead of flex.

  3. 03

    Direct-drive wheel

    Quick release, integrated display and race controls. Torque delivered straight to the shaft, with nothing lost between the track and your hands.

  1. 04

    Hand-molded body

    A complete single-seater bodyshell, laminated and finished in automotive paint. Color, livery and identity made to order.

  2. 05

    Electronics and control

    Computer, controllers and wiring harness built inside the body. One button starts it — no improvised rig, no cables in sight.

  3. 06

    Base and anchoring

    A premium-finished support platform with levelling and damping — it protects your floor and keeps the geometry stable.

10 FAQ

Before you ask.

How much does a One Grid Formula cost?

From R$ 299,000. The final figure depends on the configuration: motion, screens, finish, paint and cockpit items. The proposal is built around your project.

How much space do I need?

We define that in the design stage, using the measurements of your room. In practice, most residential installations take up an area comparable to a single garage bay, screens included.

Does it need any special installation?

A level floor and a dedicated power outlet cover most cases. We survey everything before production, and our team handles installation and calibration on site.

What can I drive on it?

It runs the leading simulation titles on the market — formula cars, prototypes, GT and touring cars, on circuits worldwide. It arrives configured, and we train you on it.

Can I customize the color and livery?

Yes. Automotive paint in the color you choose, with livery, numbering and your own identity or a sponsor's. It is part of the project, not an afterthought.

Do you deliver internationally?

Yes, on request. We organize logistics, installation and training together with you, wherever the machine is going.
